Advocate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Advocate helps parents of children with an IEP understand their rights, keep track of dates and prepare for school meetings. This policy explains what information Advocate handles, why, who else processes it, and how to delete it. Questions: realworldtraining16@gmail.com.

Who uses Advocate

Advocate is used by parents and guardians who have agreed to use it. Information about a child is provided by the child's parent or guardian. Advocate is not designed for use by children.

Information Advocate handles

  • What parents tell Advocate: questions and messages, and facts about the child such as age, grade, school, services and upcoming meetings.
  • Documents parents add: for example IEPs, evaluations, school notices, progress reports and medical reports, and notes or transcripts of meetings.
  • Emails in the family's Advocate inbox: emails the parents forward or copy to the inbox they set up for Advocate. Advocate reads that inbox only; it never sends, moves or deletes email.
  • Parents' email addresses and names, to recognise who sent an email and to share the family's calendar and notes with each parent.

Information from Google

If a family connects its Google account, Advocate asks for these permissions and uses them only as described:

  • Google Calendar (calendars Advocate creates): Advocate creates its own calendar for the child and adds, updates and removes events on that calendar only. It cannot see or change the family's other calendars or events.
  • Google Contacts: Advocate creates contacts for school staff and providers under its own label and updates only contacts it created. It does not copy, change or delete the family's other contacts.
  • Google Drive and Docs (files Advocate creates): Advocate creates a notes document for the child, adds dated entries, and shares it with the parents' own Google accounts. It cannot open the family's other files.

Limited Use. Advocate's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google APIs is used only to provide these features to the family. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. People do not read it, except with the family's permission, for security purposes, or where the law requires.

How Advocate uses information

Only to provide Advocate to that family: answering their questions, remembering their child's situation, working out dates, preparing for meetings, and keeping their calendar, contacts and notes up to date. Advocate does not show ads and does not sell or rent information. One family's information is kept in that family's own databases, separate from every other family's.

Who else processes information

  • Anthropic (the Claude AI model) reads the text and page images Advocate sends it in order to answer questions and read documents. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this information is not used to train its models.
  • DataStax Astra DB (a cloud database) stores the text of the family's documents and memory so Advocate can search it.
  • The Advocate server, a computer run by Advocate's administrator, runs Advocate and keeps the family's structured records in a private database.
  • Google, in the family's own Google account, holds the calendar, contacts, notes document and the Advocate inbox.

Advocate does not share information with schools, districts or anyone else unless the parent chooses to send something themselves.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Each family's databases have their own logins that cannot reach another family's data. Access to the Advocate server is limited to its administrator.

Keeping and deleting information

  • Information is kept while the family uses Advocate.
  • Chat messages are cleared from the chat screen after a day; what Advocate learned from them stays in the family's memory until deleted.
  • Uploaded files are deleted once read; their text is kept in the family's own databases.
  • A parent can delete the whole family at any time ("delete my family"). That removes the family's databases. Nightly backups are kept for 7 days and then deleted.
  • A family can disconnect Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The calendar, contacts and notes Advocate created stay in the family's account, where the family can keep or delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and families are told about important changes before they apply.
