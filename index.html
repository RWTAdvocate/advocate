Advocate

A private helper for parents of children with an IEP (special education).

What Advocate does

  • Answers a parent's questions about special education rights, using federal law (IDEA) and the parent's state rules, with the source quoted.
  • Reads the family's own documents (IEPs, evaluations, notices, progress reports) and emails about the child, and remembers what matters.
  • Works out important dates and deadlines, and helps parents prepare for school meetings.
  • With the family's permission, keeps a calendar, a list of the school team's contacts and a notes document in the family's own Google account.

How Advocate uses your Google account

Only if a family chooses to connect it, and only for these features:

  • Calendar: Advocate creates its own calendar (for example "Advocate – Sam") and adds meetings and deadlines to it. It cannot see or change any other calendar.
  • Contacts: Advocate adds school staff and providers under its own label (for example "Advocate – Sam team") and updates only the contacts it created.
  • Docs and Drive: Advocate creates a notes document for the family and works only with files it created.

Families can disconnect Advocate at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Important

Advocate is an information and organizing tool. It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. For high-stakes matters it suggests speaking with a qualified professional.
